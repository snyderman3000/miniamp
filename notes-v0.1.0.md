First release of MiniAmp, a classic-skin music player for the Miyoo Mini Plus (OnionOS).

- Plays MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and WAV
- Loads classic `.wsz` skins (put them in `MiniAmp/skins`), with a skin browser that previews as you scroll; ships with the original "Graphite" skin
- Music library browser, playlists (`.m3u`), shuffle, repeat, sleep timer, resume where you left off
- 10-band equalizer with presets, including "Small Speaker" for the built-in speaker
- Spectrum or oscilloscope in the main window, five full-screen visualizers
- Screen-off playback (Options > Turn screen off; START pauses, L/R skip)

Install: copy `App/MiniAmp` to the SD card, or install it with Mixtape.
